What you get

Everything the counter needs, nothing it doesn't.

Built as a single installable app that runs from your browser and keeps working when the line goes down.

Instant, even offline

Records are written to a local database on the device. No round trip, no spinner, and the till keeps selling with the network unplugged.

Three roles, one rule

Admins run the shop, managers add stock, cashiers sell. Permissions are checked on every action, not just hidden in the menu.

Receipts that print

80 mm thermal layouts for cash, card and mobile-money payments, plus a printable sales report when you need the day on paper.

Stock you can trust

Quantities drop as you sell, low-stock alerts fire before a shelf runs dry, and barcode scanning is built into the search.

Reports that add up

Revenue, profit and best-sellers calculated the same way everywhere, so the closing figure always matches the receipts.

Back up in one click

Export every user, product, sale and setting to a single file, and restore it on a new device whenever you need to.
